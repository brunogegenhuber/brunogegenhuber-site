01ABOUT

How does the brain take shape?

I am a postdoctoral fellow in Michael Greenberg's laboratory in the Department of Neurobiology at Harvard Medical School. I am also an instructor in the Advanced Techniques in Molecular Neuroscience Course at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory.

Before Harvard, I earned my PhD at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, where I discovered how steroid hormones regulate neuronal gene expression to generate sex differences in the brain.

I am especially interested in leveraging fundamental biology to find new ways to re-open brain plasticity for treating disease.

02RESEARCH

Research interests.

01 /

Brain plasticity & critical period closure

What determines how and when the developing brain loses plasticity, and can we target these pathways to restore plasticity in adulthood? My recent work implicates astrocyte glucocorticoid receptor signaling in closing critical periods in development.

↗
02 /

Glial endocrine sensing

Astrocytes are the gateway to the brain. How do astrocytes sense and respond to the multitude of circulating factors in the blood to alter neuronal function and behavior?

↗
03 /

Molecular basis of trauma

How can single-cell cell-type-resolved genomic approaches reveal the fundamental principles by which stress alters brain maturation, function, and behavior?
